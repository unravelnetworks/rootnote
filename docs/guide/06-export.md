# Export and solve problems

**Check & export** looks over the report and makes the files you deliver: a Word report, a CSV of the findings, or a PDF.

## Check before you export

Choose **Check & export** at the top right. It lists things you may want to look at, such as a missing caption or a finding without a CVSS score. **Fix** (1) takes you straight to each one.

![The Check & export list with the first Fix button marked 1.](__IMG_CHECKS__)

Most items do not stop the export. A few problems do, such as a missing screenshot; the page then says **Cannot export yet** and what to fix. The checks catch omissions, but they cannot tell whether the report is right, so read it before you send it.

## Word report

1. Choose **A4** or **Letter**.
2. Choose **Save Word report…** or **Export Word report** (1), depending on your browser.

![The paper size buttons and the Export Word report button, marked 1.](__IMG_EXPORT__)

The file is made on your computer; nothing is uploaded. Open it in Word, check the page breaks and make your final edits there. Edits in Word do not go back into Rootnote.

## Word and the preview differ

The preview is close to the Word file, but Word lays out the pages itself, so page breaks can move. Always open the Word file once before you send it.

## Findings as CSV

**Findings as CSV** makes a spreadsheet with one row per finding, for example to track fixes. Images are not included: each screenshot is replaced by text with its ID and caption.

## PDF

1. Choose **Print or save as PDF** in **Check & export**, or **Print / Save as PDF…** in the preview.
2. In the print window, choose *Save as PDF* as the printer.
3. Turn off **Headers and footers**.

> **Warning:** With headers and footers on, the browser prints the page address on every page. For the downloaded copy that is a folder path on your computer, which can include your user name.

In Firefox, links inside the PDF are printed as plain text. The Word file keeps its links.

## Common problems

- **The file will not open:** the message says why; your current work was not changed.
- **A screenshot was not added:** it must be PNG or JPEG and up to about 2 MB. See [Add a screenshot](../screenshots/#add-a-screenshot).
- **Pasting does nothing:** open a finding first, and copy an image rather than a file.
- **The browser asks whether the page may edit files:** answer the question at the top of the window; the save finishes after that.
- **The engagement list is empty:** see [One folder per engagement](../files/#one-folder-per-engagement).
- **Word asks to repair the file:** please tell us at support@unravelnetworks.com, with steps to repeat it using made-up data. Never send client reports or screenshots.
