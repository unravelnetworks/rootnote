# Add and edit screenshots

Screenshots are your evidence. Rootnote keeps them inside the project file, removes hidden data from them, and lets you black out what must not leave.

## Add a screenshot

Open a finding first: a screenshot belongs to the finding that is open.

- **Paste** it: for example Win+Shift+S, then Ctrl+V.
- **Drop** PNG or JPEG files onto the finding.
- Choose **Screenshot** in the insert bar between blocks.

You can add up to 400 screenshots to a report, each up to 2 MB, as long as the project file stays within 256 MB. If one is not added, the message says why.

## Caption it

Every screenshot gets a figure number in the report. Type a short **caption** (1) next to it so the reader knows what it shows.

![A screenshot in a finding, with the caption box marked 1 and Black out or crop marked 2.](__IMG_SCREENSHOT_CARD__)

## Black out secrets

1. Choose **Black out or crop…** (2) under the screenshot.
2. Choose **Black out** (1) and drag over the password, token or personal data.
3. Choose **Save changes**.

![The screenshot editor with the Black out tool marked 1.](__IMG_REDACT__)

The editor also has **Pixelate**, **Box**, **Arrow** and **Crop**. You can use a mouse, a pen, a finger or the keyboard.

> **Warning:** Use **Black out** for secrets, not Pixelate: pixelated text can sometimes be read back. Check the edited screenshot, and the exported report, before you send it.

Right after an edit, **Undo** can bring the original back, but only while the Undo offer is shown. Once you save, the project file holds only the edited picture. Copies you made earlier, and the original screenshot file, still hold the original: delete them if your engagement rules require it.

## Tall screenshots

A screenshot taller than a page, such as a full-page capture, is split across pages and keeps one figure number. If you would rather have one small overview, choose **shrink to fit** under the screenshot. Nothing is ever cut off.

## Hidden data is removed

Image files can carry hidden data, such as camera details, location and comments. Rootnote removes it automatically. It cannot find secrets that are visible in the picture: that is what Black out is for.
