# Design the report

Two pages decide how the Word report looks: **Details & cover** for the cover and branding, and **Sections & scoring** for the structure. Use **Preview report** to see each change.

## Title, client and cover

In **Details & cover**, fill in the report title, client, tester, version, engagement ID and the classification printed in the footer (for example *Confidential*). Finding IDs are built from the engagement ID, for example `UNR-2026-014-3`.

## Logos

Open **Page, cover and logos** in **Details & cover**.

![Your logo marked 1 and Client logo marked 2, each with Replace and Remove buttons and where to show it.](__IMG_LOGOS__)

- **Your logo** (1) and **Client logo** (2) are set separately, so your branding works with any client.
- Under **Show it on**, choose the cover, the page header or the page footer.
- The same section has the paper size and the cover page switch.

## How findings are rated

In **Sections & scoring**, choose **Scoring** (1): severity, risk or CVSS 3.1, alone or together.

![The Scoring choice marked 1.](__IMG_SECTIONS__)

The report's *How findings are rated* section explains your method to the reader. It is text you can edit, so make sure it matches how you rated the findings.

## Choose and order the sections

Lower on the same page is the list of report sections.

![The list of report sections, with a Show box marked 1 and a move arrow marked 2.](__IMG_SECTION_LIST__)

- Clear **Show** (1) to leave a section out. Its text stays saved.
- Use the arrows (2), or drag, to change the order.
- Type in a section's box to rename it.

A second list does the same for the sections inside each finding. You can also add up to 10 sections of your own, such as *Methodology*, in **Details & cover**.

## In Word

The report uses real Word styles, so it is easy to restyle in Word. Each finding starts on a new page. After moving or deleting figures in Word, update the fields with Ctrl+A, then F9. Check the page numbers too; a footer may need updating separately.
